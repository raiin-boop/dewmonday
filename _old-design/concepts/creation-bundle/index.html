How it works

Step 1 — Category: Pick your creative field (art, design, photography, videography, content creation, music).

Step 2 — Sub-discipline: Narrow down what you actually want to do within that field. The options change based on your category.

Step 3 — Skill level: Beginner to advanced. This determines whether we recommend starter kits or pro-grade equipment.

Step 4 — Intent: Hobby, interest, side hustle, or career. This affects budget range and durability recommendations.

Results: A curated list of equipment with honest pros/cons and direct links to where you can buy each item. No affiliate links, no commissions — just honest recommendations.

Getting started in any creative field is overwhelming. You Google "best camera for beginners" and get 47 affiliate-stuffed listicles that all recommend different things. You don't know if you need a $300 setup or a $3,000 one. You don't know what matters and what's just marketing.

This quiz cuts through that. It asks what you actually want to do, how serious you are, and gives you a straight answer with trade-offs clearly listed. No hidden agendas, no sponsored picks.

Are these affiliate links?
No. Every link goes directly to the manufacturer or retailer. Dew Monday makes zero commission on any recommendation.
How often is the equipment list updated?
Quarterly. When better options come out or prices change significantly, the recommendations update. Updates are announced in the Dew Monday newsletter.
Can I retake the quiz with different answers?
Yes — hit the retake button at the bottom of your results or use the back button during the quiz to change any answer.
What if my discipline isn't listed?
More sub-disciplines are being added as the concept develops. Subscribe to the newsletter to know when new categories drop.
