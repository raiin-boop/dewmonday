# Product Marketing Context - Art Dropshipping

> **One-liner:** Limited-time artist drops that make collecting independent art feel immediate, trustworthy, and culturally relevant.

---

## Positioning Statement

Art Dropshipping is a creator-first art commerce platform that combines limited-edition urgency with transparent artist partnerships. It sits between mass POD storefronts and high-friction gallery buying.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Mindset | Purchase Driver | Typical Basket |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emerging Collectors | "I want original-feeling pieces without gallery prices" | Limited release urgency + artist story | 1-2 prints |
| Gift Buyers | "I need something thoughtful and unique" | Curation confidence + shipping clarity | 1 framed print |
| Interior Stylers | "I need coordinated visuals with character" | Themed collections + quality consistency | 2-4 prints |
| Artist Supporters | "I want to directly support independent artists" | Revenue-share transparency | 1 print per drop |

---

## Differentiators

| Art Dropshipping | Typical POD Store |
|---|---|
| Time-bound curated drops | Always-on infinite catalog |
| Artist-forward storytelling | Product-first listings |
| Transparent collaboration model | Opaque creator economics |
| Campaign trailer per drop | Static product uploads |
| Cohesive Dew Monday editorial curation | Generic marketplace feel |

---

## Drop Model

1. Pre-drop teaser window (3-5 days): artist intro, concept preview, email waitlist.
2. Drop live window (72 hours): limited SKUs, framed/unframed options, clear countdown.
3. Close and produce: orders routed to POD partners and fulfillment tracking shared.
4. Post-drop archive: sold-out gallery with artist profile and future waitlist capture.

Drop cadence starts bi-weekly, then scales to weekly once fulfillment reliability and conversion targets are stable.

---

## Channels (Priority Order)

1. Instagram and TikTok trailers for artist reveal and drop countdown.
2. Email list for early access and waitlist conversion.
3. Pinterest boards for home decor and style discovery.
4. SEO for long-tail purchase intent (limited edition prints, independent artist wall art).
5. Partner placements with creator newsletters and design communities.

---

## Revenue Model

- POD margin on Dew Monday-owned drops.
- Revenue share model on artist collaborations.
- Premium framed option upsell.
- Optional collector bundles for multi-print sets.

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Waitlist-to-purchase conversion | 12%+ |
| Sell-through within 72 hours | 65%+ |
| Average order value | $75+ |
| Repeat customer rate (90 days) | 20%+ |
| Artist retention for second drop | 50%+ |
