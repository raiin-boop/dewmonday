# Product Marketing Context — Art Dropshipping

> **One-liner:** A curated print store that treats buyers like collectors and artists like partners.

---

## Problem

Etsy is noisy. Redbubble is mass-market. Society6 feels like a template. Buyers who care about original art can't find a shop that feels intentional, and artists who make great work don't have a storefront that tells their story.

---

## Positioning Statement

Dew Monday Art is a curated print store that puts story first — every piece comes with the artist's context, every drop is limited, and the shop is edited down to only work we'd hang on our own walls.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Age | Mindset | Trigger Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intentional Home Decorator | 24–38 | "I want my space to feel like me, not like a catalog" | Moving, redecorating, first owned home |
| Gift Buyer | 25–45 | "I want to give something meaningful, not generic" | Birthday, housewarming, holiday |
| Artist Collector | 22–40 | "I follow artists online and want to own their work" | Discovers a new artist they love |

---

## Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|---|---|
| Curated — max 60 designs in shop at once | Thousands of listings, no curation |
| Artist story on every product page | Artist name buried in fine print |
| Limited edition drops (72-hour window) | Always available, no urgency |
| 25% revenue share to artist partners | 10–15% industry standard |
| Remotion video trailer for every drop | Static product photos |

---

## Drop Model

Each collection drop follows a 72-hour window:

1. **Teaser** — 48 hours before: artist intro + 1 preview piece (email + Instagram)
2. **Drop** — 72-hour sale window with countdown timer on site
3. **Close** — edition closes, unsold inventory never reprinted
4. **Recap** — sell-through stats shared with artist, community highlight

Every drop gets a **Remotion-generated video trailer** — 15–30 seconds, branded motion graphics with the artist's work.

---

## Channels (Priority Order)

1. **Email list** — primary revenue driver, drop announcements
2. **Instagram** — artist features, studio process, drop teasers
3. **Pinterest** — room mockups, "art for [room/style]" boards
4. **Artist audiences** — co-promotion with featured artists to their followers
5. **TikTok** — studio visits, unboxing, "how this was made" process videos

---

## Artist Partnership Model

- **Revenue share:** 25% of net sale to artist (above industry standard)
- **Contract:** per-drop agreement, artist retains full IP rights
- **Promotion:** Dew Monday promotes across all channels, artist promotes to their audience
- **Creative control:** artist approves all mockups, copy, and video before publish
