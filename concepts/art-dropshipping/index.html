About Art Drops

1
We find the artist
Independent artists doing consistent, serious work — usually with a following that wants to buy and no clean way to do it. We reach out, learn about the work, and decide if it's worth a spotlight.
2
We write the spotlight
A full editorial feature — artist background, the work itself, why it matters, and what makes it worth your wall space. Not a press release. An honest piece of writing about the art.
3
We send you directly to them
Every link goes to the artist's own shop, Etsy, or direct contact. We don't handle transactions, don't take a cut, and have no commercial relationship with featured artists. What you spend goes to the person who made the work.
4
New artist every month
One spotlight per month. Subscribers get early access 48 hours before it goes public — and a short note in the Monday newsletter when it drops.
Who we're looking for
We spotlight independent artists — no gallery representation required, no minimum following. What matters is the work. Here's what we look for:
  • Consistent body of work — not just one good piece
  • Has a way to sell directly (own site, Etsy, commissions)
  • Doing original work, not reproduction or prints of others' art
  • Active — posting, creating, or taking commissions
  • Not currently represented by a commercial gallery
Submit a nomination →

Being featured in Art Drops means a full editorial spotlight on your work — written honestly, published to the Dew Monday audience, and sent in the newsletter. We write the piece, you approve it before it goes live.

We don't ask for exclusivity. We don't take a percentage of sales. We don't require you to list with us or change how you sell. We write about your work, link to where you already sell it, and get out of the way.

If you want to be considered, the best path is to be nominated by someone in the audience — or reach out directly through the newsletter reply.
