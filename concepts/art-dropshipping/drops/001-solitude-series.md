# Solitude Series — Mara Voss

Six prints. One artist. All gone when they're gone.

<div class="dm-pull-quote">"I've been making work about being alone in rooms for eight years. It took me until last year to realize that's the work."</div>

Mara Voss works in oil pastel on toned paper, then digitizes and adjusts in Procreate before sending to print. The result sits somewhere between illustration and painting — warm, slightly grainy, unmistakably human. She sells originals once a year at a small show in her city. The rest of the year, she posts on Instagram to 6,200 people who keep asking where they can buy prints.

This is where.

---

## The Artist

<div class="dm-spotlight">
  <div class="dm-spotlight-label">✦ Artist Profile</div>
  <div class="dm-spotlight-name">Mara Voss — @maravoss.studio</div>
  <p class="dm-spotlight-body">Based in Minneapolis. Works out of a converted sun porch with north-facing light she describes as "the only good thing about Minnesota winters." Day job in graphic design. Has been making personal work since 2018 — oil pastel first, then digital integration starting in 2022 when a broken wrist meant she couldn't hold a pastel stick for four months and had to figure out another way.<br><br>The Solitude Series started as a pandemic project and became the body of work she's most proud of. Six pieces made over three years. Each one is a different room. Each room has one person, or the feeling of one person who just left.</p>
  <div class="dm-spotlight-quote">"The rooms aren't sad. They're just honest about what it feels like to be the only one in them."</div>
</div>
