# Product Marketing Context — Creator Hub

> **One-liner:** Makes you a better creator one Monday at a time.

---

## Problem

Creators are scattered across Discord servers, Reddit threads, Slack groups, and newsletters. No single place combines genuinely useful resources, real peer connection, and a brand that understands the creator lifestyle. Most platforms are either too corporate (LinkedIn) or too chaotic (Discord with 47 channels).

---

## Positioning Statement

Dew Monday Creator Hub gives you one genuinely useful thing every week — a vetted resource, a real conversation, or a creative prompt that actually makes you better. Not another feed to scroll. Not another Slack to mute.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Age | Mindset | Trigger Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo Creator Building in Public | 22–35 | "I want peers, not followers" | Feels isolated creating alone |
| Side Hustler | 25–40 | "I need to figure out the business side" | Gets first paid client, realizes they need pricing/contract help |
| Student / Early Creator | 16–24 | "I'm learning and want to share the journey" | Finishes Art Tutorial App track, wants community |

---

## Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|---|---|
| One well-edited newsletter per week | Daily firehose of notifications |
| Vetted resource library (< 50 tools, each reviewed) | Crowdsourced link dumps with no curation |
| Founding member tier with lifetime access | Subscription treadmill with no loyalty reward |
| Content pillars: Craft, Business, Community, Gear | Random topic mix with no editorial structure |
| Dew Monday voice — warm, honest, specific | Corporate "creator economy" jargon |

---

## Newsletter Format

Each weekly edition follows this structure (600–800 words):

1. **Opening** — one thought on the creative life (3–4 sentences)
2. **The Gear Take** — one honest product opinion (ties to Camera Dropshipping)
3. **Three Resources** — vetted tools, templates, or guides with one-line reviews
4. **The Work** — one creator spotlight or community project highlight
5. **One Prompt** — a creative exercise for the week

---

## Community Seeding Strategy

**Phase 1 — Founding 50:**
- Hand-pick 50 creators from Twitter/Instagram who fit the ICP
- Personal invite email from Dew Monday (not a mass blast)
- Founding member lifetime access ($149 one-time)
- These 50 set the culture and tone

**Phase 2 — Controlled Growth:**
- Open waitlist, accept in batches of 25
- Each new member gets a welcome thread
- Community stays invite-only until 200 members

## Ecosystem Connections

- **Camera Dropshipping:** Gear Take section in newsletter features one product per week
- **Art Dropshipping:** Artist partners are featured in The Work section
- **Art Tutorial App:** Students who complete a track get invited to community
- **Little Makers:** Family-friendly creative prompts shared as bonus content
