# Product Marketing  Creator Hub

Most creator communities are either a dead Discord or a firehose newsletter. Creator Hub is one well-edited thing, every Monday. Real value, no noise.

---

## Positioning Statement

Dew Monday Creator Hub gives you one genuinely useful thing every week  a vetted resource, a real conversation, or a creative prompt that actually makes you better. Not another feed to scroll. Not another Slack to mute.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Age | Mindset | Trigger Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo Creator Building in Public | 2235 | "I want peers, not followers" | Feels isolated creating alone |
| Side Hustler | 2540 | "I need to figure out the business side" | Gets first paid client, needs pricing/contract help |
| Student / Early Creator | 1624 | "I'm learning and want to share the journey" | Finishes Art Tutorial App track, wants community |

---

## Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|---|---|
| One well-edited newsletter per week | Daily firehose of notifications |
| Vetted resource library (> 50 tools, each reviewed) | Crowdsourced link dumps with no curation |
| Founding member tier with lifetime access | Subscription treadmill with no loyalty reward |
| Content pillars: Craft, Business, Community, Gear | Random topic mix with no editorial structure |
| Dew Monday voice  warm, honest, specific | Corporate "creator economy" jargon |

---

## Newsletter Format

Each weekly edition follows this structure (600800 words):

1. **Opening**  one thought on the creative life (34 sentences)
2. 2. **The Gear Take**  one honest product opinion (ties to Camera Dropshipping)
   3. 3. **Three Resources**  vetted tools, templates, or guides with one-line reviews
      4. 4. **The Work**  one creator spotlight or community project highlight
         5. 5. **One Prompt**  a creative exercise for the week
           
            6. ---
           
            7. ## Community Seeding Strategy
           
            8. **Phase 1  Founding 50:**
            9. - Hand-pick 50 creators from Twitter/Instagram who fit the ICP
               - - DM with a personal note (no templates)
                 - - Offer lifetime access in exchange for founding feedback
                  
                   - **Phase 2  First 500:**
                   - - Waitlist page live (already built  Creator Hub waitlist app)
                     - - Weekly newsletter drops publicly archived on site
                       - - Cross-promote via Art Tutorial App community
                        
                         - **Phase 3  Open:**
                         - - Public community with paid tier unlocking live sessions + direct feedback
