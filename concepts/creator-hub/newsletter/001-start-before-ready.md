# Start Before You're Ready

There's a version of this newsletter that I planned for six months. I had a Notion doc, a content calendar, a brand guide, a posting schedule. I had a logo in three sizes. I had every reason to keep planning.

Then I remembered something a photographer friend told me: *"The camera you have with you is the best camera."* Not the one on the wishlist. The one in your hand, today.

This first edition is that camera.

<div class="dm-pull-quote">"The right moment is a myth, and Monday morning is close enough."</div>

You're reading this because something in you wants to make things — and maybe because you've also been waiting for the right moment to start. The difference between people who make things and people who *want* to make things usually isn't talent, money, or connections. It's a willingness to produce something unfinished and let it be seen.

Every creator I genuinely admire has a trail of awkward early work they're slightly embarrassed by. That trail is proof they showed up before they were ready.

So here's what I want for you this week: one small, real thing. Not the full project. One piece of it. A paragraph, a sketch, a single photo, a rough voice note. Something that exists in the world instead of only in the plan.

We'll build from there, every Monday.
