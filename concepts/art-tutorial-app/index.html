How It Works

Structured, not rigid. A path that bends to how you work.

Step 01 🗺️
Choose a track

Pick based on your medium, your skill level, or what kind of work you want to make. No quiz. No algorithm. Just a clear description of where each path goes.

Step 02 🎬
Work through lessons

Video lessons you can pause, rewind, and work alongside. Downloadable project files included. Each lesson builds on the last and ends with something to make.

Step 03 📤
Submit your project

Every track ends in a real project. Upload it to the community gallery, get feedback from other learners, and keep it for your portfolio.

Step 04 🔁
Go deeper

Stack tracks, try a new medium, or come back to refine. The goal is momentum — not streaks, not points, just work you're genuinely proud of.
