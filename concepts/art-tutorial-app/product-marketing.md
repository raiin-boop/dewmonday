# Product Marketing Context — Art Tutorial App

> **One-liner:** A project-first learning platform that makes creative education feel like an invitation, not a chore.

---

## Problem

YouTube is free but unstructured — you watch 40 tutorials and still can't draw a hand. Skillshare is broad but has no spine — no clear progression, no accountability. Most art education platforms feel corporate or gamified in ways that miss the actual creative process.

---

## Positioning Statement

Dew Monday Tutorials is structured creative education with a shareable project at the end of every single lesson. You don't just learn — you make something, share it, and move forward.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Age | Mindset | Trigger Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner With Intent | 16–28 | "I want to actually learn this, not just watch videos" | Tries to draw something and realizes they need structure |
| Creator Upgrader | 22–35 | "My content is good but my visuals could be better" | Compares their work to creators they admire |
| Hobbyist | 25–55 | "I want to learn something creative for myself" | Looking for a fulfilling non-work activity |

---

## Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|---|---|
| Every lesson ends in a shareable project | Lessons end in "now practice on your own" |
| Structured tracks with clear progression | Disconnected individual classes |
| Public project gallery (built-in portfolio) | No showcase beyond the platform |
| Remotion-branded lesson intros | Generic video packaging |
| Dew Monday voice — warm, specific, never condescending | Corporate or overly casual |

---

## Onboarding Flow

```
Signup (email or Google)
  → Creative quiz: "What do you want to make?" (3 questions)
    → Recommended track (Digital Illustration, Photography, etc.)
      → Lesson 1 unlocked immediately (no paywall)
        → Project submitted to gallery
          → Day 2 email: "Here's what you made. Ready for lesson 2?"
```

---

## Paywall Design

- **Lessons 1–3 of every track:** Free (no account required to browse, account required to save progress)
- **Paywall trigger:** Lesson 4 — by this point the learner has invested effort and completed 3 projects
- **Pricing:** $14/month or $120/year (individual) — positioned below Skillshare, above free

---

## Channels (Priority Order)

1. **SEO** — "how to draw [subject] step by step" long-tail
2. **TikTok UGC** — learner project showcases, "lesson 1 vs lesson 10" progression
3. **Creator partnerships** — instructors promote their own tracks
4. **Email** — weekly "new lesson" and progress nudges
5. **Referral** — "invite a friend, both get a free month"
6. **App Store** — ASO for "art tutorial" and "learn to draw" keywords

---

## Curriculum Track Priority

1. **Digital Illustration** (Procreate / Photoshop) — highest search volume, broadest appeal
2. **Photography Composition** — ties to Camera Dropshipping concept
3. **Color Theory** — foundational, applies across all media
4. **Video Editing** — growing demand, ties to creator audience
