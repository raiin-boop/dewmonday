# Product Marketing  Art Tutorial App

YouTube is free but unstructured  you watch 40 tutorials and still can't draw a hand. Skillshare is broad but has no spine  no clear progression, no accountability. Most art education platforms feel corporate or gamified in ways that miss the actual creative process.

---

## Positioning Statement

Dew Monday Tutorials is structured creative education with a shareable project at the end of every single lesson. You don't just learn  you make something, share it, and move forward.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Age | Mindset | Trigger Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner With Intent | 1628 | "I want to actually learn this, not just watch videos" | Tries to draw something and realizes they need structure |
| Creator Upgrader | 2235 | "My content is good but my visuals could be better" | Compares their work to creators they admire |
| Hobbyist | 2555 | "I want to learn something creative for myself" | Looking for a fulfilling non-work activity |

---

## Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|---|---|
| Every lesson ends in a shareable project | Lessons end in "now practice on your own" |
| Structured tracks with clear progression | Disconnected individual classes |
| Public project gallery (built-in portfolio) | No showcase beyond the platform |
| Remotion-branded lesson intros | Generic video packaging |
| Dew Monday voice  warm, specific, never condescending | Corporate or overly casual |

---

## Onboarding Flow

```
Signup (email or Google)
 Creative quiz: "What do you want to make?" (3 questions)
 Recommended track lands on dashboard
 Free preview lesson (Lesson 1 of any track)
 Paywall after Lesson 1
```

---

## Paywall Design

| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Lesson 1 of every track, project gallery browsing |
| Monthly | $12/mo | All tracks, project submissions, community feed |
| Annual | $99/yr | Everything monthly + early access to new tracks |
