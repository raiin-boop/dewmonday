Activity Guides

Every activity is tagged by age range. These are starting points — kids develop at different rates. When in doubt, start easier and let them tell you when they're ready for more.

Ages 3–5
Explorers
  • Sensory and open-ended play
  • Large motor movements
  • Simple mixing and pouring
  • 10–20 minute attention spans
  • Adult present throughout
Ages 6–8
Builders
  • Can follow 2–3 step instructions
  • Basic cutting and gluing
  • Color mixing and simple art
  • 20–35 minute projects
  • Light supervision needed
Ages 8–10
Makers
  • Multi-step projects
  • Problem-solving and iteration
  • Basic measuring and math
  • 30–60 minute projects
  • Can work more independently
Ages 10–12
Creators
  • Complex multi-day projects
  • Writing and planning
  • Real tools with supervision
  • 45+ minute deep focus
  • Mostly independent

Little Makers exists because the internet is full of activity ideas that require a trip to a specialty store, a Pinterest-level photo setup, or a degree in early childhood education to execute. Most of them are designed to look impressive rather than to actually work.

Every activity here is designed to work with what most families already have. The instructions are written for parents, not bloggers — direct, step-by-step, and honest about what might go wrong and what age range it actually suits.
