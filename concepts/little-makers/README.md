# 🌱 Little Makers

> **Concept Status:** 🌱 Ideation
> **Last Updated:** June 2026

---

## Overview

A free creative activity hub for kids ages 0–8, built for the adults in their lives — parents, babysitters, grandparents, and teachers. Little Makers provides structured, age-appropriate creative activities using materials already found in most households. No craft store runs required.

---

## Problem Statement

Parents and caregivers hit three walls repeatedly:

1. **Ideas run dry** — you can only do finger painting so many times before everyone's bored
2. **Supplies cost money** — most activity sites assume you have a Michael's budget and a Pinterest-ready craft room
3. **Resources assume too much** — instructions are written for adults with art degrees, not a babysitter with 45 minutes and a toddler

---

## Value Proposition

- **Accessible by design** — every activity has a household-only version using things you already own
- **Age-filtered** — activities tagged for 0–2, 2–4, 4–6, and 6–8 so you're not guessing
- **Educational but fun** — each activity lists the developmental skill it supports (fine motor, color recognition, sensory, etc.)
- **Quick** — most activities take 10–30 minutes, because that's the real attention span
- **No account, no paywall** — the core experience is free, forever

---

## Who It's For

| Segment | Profile |
|---|---|
| Parents | Daily caregivers looking for "what do we do now?" answers |
| Babysitters | Need quick, reliable activities with whatever's in the house |
| Grandparents | Want to engage meaningfully during visits without a supply run |
| Teachers | PreK and kindergarten teachers looking for low-prep activities |
| Family Members | Aunts, uncles, older siblings — anyone watching a kid for a few hours |

---

## Core Features

**MVP:**
- 50+ activities at launch
- Filter by age group, available materials, time needed, and developmental skill
- Each activity card includes: what you need, steps, why it helps, messiness rating (1–5)
- Tool and toy recommendations with free household alternatives for each
- "Surprise Me" button — random activity based on your filters

**V2:**
- Save favorites (optional free account)
- Weekly email: "5 activities for this week" based on age preference
- Seasonal activity packs (holiday crafts, summer projects, rainy day specials)
- Printable activity cards for offline use

**V3:**
- Community-submitted activities (moderated)
- Multi-language support / translations
- Art Tutorial App integration — "grown-up version" of kid activities for parents who want to learn too

---

## Accessibility Commitment (Non-Negotiable)

These five rules are core to the concept and cannot be compromised:

1. **Every activity has a household-only version** — if we recommend paint, we also show how to do it with food coloring and water
2. **No account required, ever** — browse and use everything without signing up
3. **No ads targeting kids** — if we monetize with ads, they appear in caregiver-facing sections only
4. **Printable for offline use** — every activity can be printed as a simple card
5. **Plain language** — instructions written at a 7th-grade reading level, max 4 steps per activity

## Revenue Model

| Source | Share | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate links | 60% | Tool and toy recommendations (Amazon Associates, etc.) — always with free alternative listed first |
| Seasonal PDF packs | 25% | Curated downloadable activity collections ($3–5 each) |
| Brand partnerships | 15% | Kid-safe brand sponsorships (art supplies, educational toys) — vetted, never intrusive |
