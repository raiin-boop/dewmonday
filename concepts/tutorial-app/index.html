Instructor model decided: practitioners only
No course factories, no ghost-written content. Every instructor must be actively working in their discipline. Revenue split: 60% instructor / 40% platform. First instructor conversations happening this week.

Week 1
Concept validated — problem is real, market is crowded
Skillshare, Domestika, and YouTube are the main competitors. The gap: none of them are built specifically for independent creators building a real creative business. That's the positioning.
validationcompetitive
When does it launch?
Target is Q3 2026 for a closed beta with Dew Monday subscribers. Public launch Q4. The build is happening in public every week — subscribe to follow along.
What makes this different from Skillshare or YouTube?
Structure and outcome focus. YouTube is free but disorganized. Skillshare is broad but shallow. This platform is built around specific skills with a clear end state — you finish a course and you can do something you couldn't before.
Can I teach on the platform?
Applications open Q2 2026. Requirements: actively working in your discipline, 60-minute minimum course, structured around a specific outcome. Subscribe for updates on the instructor program.
