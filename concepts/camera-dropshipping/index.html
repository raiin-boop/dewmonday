🎯
Built around a specific creator type
Each drop starts with a creator profile, not a product list. Drop 001 is for solo video creators upgrading from a phone. Drop 002 is for podcasters setting up their first real studio.
🔗
Everything works together
We don't curate individual products — we curate kits. The camera pairs with the lens. The mic pairs with the interface. The lighting works with the space. If it doesn't work together, it doesn't make the drop.
🚫
No affiliate links, ever
We don't earn commissions from what you buy. No sponsored placements, no paid inclusions. Gear makes the drop because it's the right gear, not because someone paid for the slot.
📐
Priced so the math works
Each kit is priced against realistic creator budgets. Drop 001 is $1,010 for the full stack. Drop 002 will be under $700. We show the full kit price upfront — no hidden add-ons.
Do you sell the gear directly?
No. Camera Drops links to manufacturer sites and authorized retailers. You buy directly from them — we're the editorial layer, not the storefront.
Do you have affiliate deals with any of these brands?
No. No affiliate links, no brand relationships, no sponsored inclusions. Everything in a drop is there because we chose it independently.
How do subscribers get early access?
Each new drop goes to the newsletter 48 hours before it goes public on the site. Subscribe at dewmonday.com — free, one email per week.
Can I suggest gear for a future drop?
Yes — reply to any newsletter issue with a suggestion. We read every reply. If we've already tested something you suggest, we'll tell you what we thought.
