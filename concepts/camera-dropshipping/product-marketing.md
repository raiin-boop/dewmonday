# Product Marketing Context — Camera Dropshipping

> **One-liner:** A curated gear store for creators who want a trusted recommendation, not 47 options.

---

## Problem

Creators are overwhelmed by choice. Every camera retailer shows hundreds of options, affiliate reviews feel bought, and comparison charts assume you already know what "IBIS" means. The result: decision paralysis, buyer's remorse, or defaulting to whatever a YouTuber was paid to promote.

---

## Positioning Statement

Dew Monday Camera acts like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know cameras — not a store that happens to have a blog. We recommend ONE camera per use case, explain why in plain language, and stand behind the pick.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Age | Mindset | Trigger Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-Timer | 18–28 | "I want to start creating but I don't know what to buy" | Gets serious about content creation |
| Upgrader | 22–35 | "My phone isn't cutting it anymore" | Hits a quality ceiling on current gear |
| Niche Enthusiast | 25–45 | "I know what I want but I want a second opinion" | Researching a specific genre (film, street, astro) |

---

## Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|---|---|
| ONE camera per use case | 47 options sorted by price |
| Plain-language "why this one" writeup | Spec sheets and star ratings |
| Editorial curation by working creators | Algorithm-sorted catalog |
| Gear + tutorial bundling | Standalone product pages |
| Honest "skip this" when a product isn't worth it | Everything gets 4+ stars |

---

## Key Messages by Audience

**First-Timer:** "You don't need to research for weeks. We already did. Here's the one camera that makes sense for what you want to do."

**Upgrader:** "You've outgrown your setup. Here's exactly what changes and what stays the same when you step up."

**Niche Enthusiast:** "We shoot this genre too. Here's what we'd actually buy with our own money."

---

## Tone & Voice (stop-slop aligned)

- Specific, not superlative ("the Fuji X-T5 handles low light well at ISO 3200" not "amazing low-light performance")
- Honest about tradeoffs — every recommendation includes a "what you give up" note
- Never use: "game-changer," "must-have," "holy grail," "absolute beast"
- Reads like a knowledgeable friend's text message, not a product listing

---

## Channels (Priority Order)

1. **SEO** — long-tail "best camera for [use case]" content
2. **Creator partnerships** — gear reviews from real users, not influencer deals
3. **Email** — weekly "one pick" newsletter
4. **Pinterest** — gear aesthetic boards, setup tours
5. **TikTok** — 60-second "this or that" comparisons

---

## Conversion Funnel

```
SEO/Social → Landing page (one pick per use case)
  → Product page (why this one + what you give up)
    → Add to cart (gear + optional tutorial bundle)
      → Checkout (Stripe)
        → Post-purchase email (setup guide + community invite)
```

## Metrics (Targets)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Conversion rate | 2.5% |
| Average order value (AOV) | $420 |
| Email open rate | 40% |
| Return rate | < 5% |
| Repeat purchase (within 6 months) | 15% |

---

## What We Are Not

- Not a marketplace — we curate, we don't list everything
- Not a review site — we recommend, we don't rank 20 options
- Not a discount store — we compete on trust, not price
- Not a brand rep — we recommend across brands based on merit
