# The Creator Starter Kit

Every month we put together one drop. Not fifty products. One kit — built around a specific creator type, tested against a specific use case, and priced so the math actually works.

This is Drop 001. We built it for the creator who has been filming on their phone, knows they're ready for more, and doesn't want to spend six hours reading spec sheets to figure out where to start.

<div class="dm-pull-quote">"The best starter kit isn't the cheapest. It's the one you won't immediately outgrow."</div>

Everything in this kit works together. The camera pairs with the lens. The lens pairs with the lighting. The audio works with the camera body. You can buy individual pieces or the whole stack — but the whole stack is how it was designed to be used.
