## What is Dew Monday?

Dew Monday is a brand incubator — a place to develop, stress-test, and ship creative business concepts with intention. Every concept shares the same design language, the same quality bar, and the same commitment to making things that are genuinely useful.

The name comes from the feeling of a quiet Monday morning: clear air, fresh start, something worth building.

## Design Philosophy

> Make it warm. Make it readable. Make it feel like it was made by a person who cares.

Every concept in this incubator shares one visual language: **warm cream surfaces, forest green accents, DM Serif Display headings, Inter body text**. The design system lives in [`brand/design-system`](brand/design-system) — token-level specs that port cleanly to any framework.
