#  Dew Monday  Brand Identity

> This folder holds brand guidelines, visual references, and identity assets for the Dew Monday brand.
>
> ---
>
> ## Brand Essence
>
> | Attribute | Description |
> |---|---|
> | **Name** | Dew Monday |
> | **Feeling** | Fresh start energy  the clarity of a quiet Monday morning |
> | **Voice** | Warm, editorial, creator-native. Not corporate. Never hype. |
> | **Mission** | Lower the barrier between creative ambition and real output |
> | **Audience** | Independent creators, artists, photographers, and makers |
>
> ---
>
> ## Brand Personality
>
> - **Curious**  always exploring, never know-it-all
> - - **Grounded**  honest about what works and what doesn't
>   - - **Generous**  shares knowledge, lifts others
>     - - **Aesthetic**  cares deeply about visual quality without being precious about it
>      
>       - ---
>
> ## Voice & Tone Guidelines
>
> ### Do
>
> - Write like a knowledgeable friend, not a salesperson
> - - Use clear, specific language ("50mm f/1.8" not "a great lens")
>   - - Celebrate process, not just outcomes
>     - - Be enthusiastic without exclamation-point inflation
>       - - Name the hard parts honestly
>        
>         - ### Don't
>        
>         - - Use growth-hacking buzzwords ("crush it", "scale", "hack")
>           - - Write in passive voice when active is available
>             - - Over-explain what the reader already knows
>               - - Fake urgency
>                
>                 - ---
